## 1. Our position, stated plainly

OPTCG YoHoHo! is an **unofficial, non-commercial fan project**. It is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or licensed by Bandai Co.,
Ltd., Bandai Namco Holdings, Shueisha Inc., Toei Animation Co., Ltd., Eiichiro

We respect intellectual property rights. We are not trying to compete with,
substitute for, or profit from anyone's product. The Service exists so that
people who already own and play the physical card game can practise and learn
it. We believe it drives interest in the official product rather than away
from it.

**We do not sell anything.** No cards, packs, currency, cosmetics, codes, or
advantages are sold. The Service is free. Voluntary donations, if accepted, go
to server hosting and buy the donor nothing.

**All third-party marks and artwork remain the property of their owners.**
Card names, card text, card images, character names, logos, and trade dress
displayed by the Service are used solely to identify the card a player is
simulating. We claim no ownership of them and grant no rights in them to
anyone.

## 2. To rights holders

**If you own rights in material used by this Service and you want it removed,
you do not need to send a formal legal notice, hire a lawyer, or contact our
hosting provider.**

Write to **support@optcgcustom.app** and tell us what you want removed. We will:

1. **Acknowledge within 3 business days.**
2. **Remove or disable the material promptly** — in most cases within 7 days
   of verifying the request, and faster where we can.
3. **Comply with a request to shut the Service down entirely**, if that is
   what is asked of us by a verified rights holder.

We would obviously rather find a middle ground — removing card artwork,
switching to text-only cards, adding attribution, changing the name, or
whatever else resolves the concern — and we will engage in good faith on any
of that. But we are not going to fight you about it, and we will not require
you to escalate.

### 3.1 What a notice must contain

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3), a valid notice must include substantially the
following:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner, or a person
   authorised to act on their behalf.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or
   a representative list if multiple works are covered by a single notice.
3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough
   detail — a URL is best — for us to locate it.
4. Your contact information: name, address, telephone number, and email.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not
   authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and, under
   penalty of perjury, that you are authorised to act on behalf of the owner.

Please note that under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), knowingly making a material
misrepresentation that material is infringing can make you liable for damages.

### 3.2 What we do with a valid notice

We remove or disable the identified material expeditiously, notify the user
who supplied it (where any user supplied it), and log the notice.

## 4. Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe that was a mistake or a
misidentification, send a counter-notice to **support@optcgcustom.app** containing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material removed and where it appeared before
   removal.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the
   material was removed as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address, and telephone number.
5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district
   court for the district where you live (or, if outside the United States,
   any district in which we may be found), and that you will accept service of
   process from the person who filed the original notice.

We may restore the material 10 to 14 business days after forwarding your
counter-notice, unless the original complainant tells us they have filed an
action seeking a court order.

## 5. Trademarks

For trademark concerns — the site name, logo, domain, presentation, or any
suggestion of official affiliation — write to **support@optcgcustom.app** describing the
mark, your rights in it, and what you want changed. We will respond on the same
timeline as § 2, and renaming or rebranding is on the table.

## 6. Repeat infringers

We terminate the accounts of users who repeatedly upload infringing material,
in appropriate circumstances. Users cannot currently upload arbitrary media,
so this applies chiefly to text fields and any future upload feature.

## 7. Abusive notices

We will comply with genuine requests without argument. We reserve the right to
ask for verification that a sender actually represents the rights holder
before acting on a request that would remove content or disable a service used
by other people.
