## 8. Match records and AI training

Finished matches are recorded in two different ways, for two different
reasons:

- **Match history** — the result of a match (who played, who won, why, when).
  This is your record of your own play and is part of operating a competitive
  service.
- **Match trajectories** — the full action stream of a match, used as research
  material for improving the computer opponent.

A trajectory covers *both* players, so it cannot be half-recorded. **Every
seat in a match must agree before a trajectory is stored.** If any player
declines, no trajectory is written for that match. You can change your setting
at any time; it applies to matches played after the change.

You may ask us to delete trajectories that involve your account. See
[Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md).
